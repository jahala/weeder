## Positioning

The judge of the diff. An agent writes a change and says it is finished; weed reads the
change and says whether it is honest. Deleted or weakened tests, skips, stubs, swallowed
errors, secrets, guardrail edits, dependency-direction violations — a static binary,
milliseconds, no tokens, every finding as SARIF 2.1.0.

**tilth** reads code so an agent can find its way; weed reads a diff so a human does not
have to take the agent's word for what it did. Both are Rust binaries that parse through
tree-sitter and cost a session nothing until they are called. The difference is the verb:
tilth answers, weed refuses.

The exit code is the product. A gate that reports and never blocks is a report, and a
report is something a tired reviewer scrolls past.

## Mark

A pair of shears open around a weed's stem, at the height where the cut is made. The plant
is growth green (`#357E2C`) — a stem, three leaves, still growing; the shears are the accent,
two crossed blades and two open handle loops, and they are the only thing in the mark that
is not alive.

The reading is the judgement, one moment before it lands: the plant is whole, the blades are
already around it, and nothing has been cut yet. That is what `weed check` is — a verdict
delivered before the change goes in, not a report on damage afterwards. Drawn in the garden's
diagram language, stroked stems and filled accent shapes, so it sits beside umbel's
inflorescence and pollen's two leaning stalks as a sibling.

Files: `assets/weed-mark.svg` (paper) · `assets/weed-mark-night.svg` (soil-night).

## Logo usage

- Minimum size: 20px mark height, rendered at 16 · 20 · 28 · 48 and read at each. At 20px the
  leaves flatten and the handle loops close up, and the shears-on-a-stem reading survives. At
  16px it does not; use the wordmark below 20.
- Clear space: half the mark height on every side.
- Fills are exact — growth green `#357E2C` and the accent on paper; on soil-night the plant
  brightens to `#84C56A` and the shears to the accent's night value (the night file). Never
  recolour outside those values.
- The blades never touch the leaves, and the stem is never drawn already cut. The shears are
  open, always. A severed stem is a different claim from the one weed makes.
- Never: colour the plant in the accent, colour the shears green, close the handle loops into
  filled dots, add a hand holding them, or outline the wordmark.
